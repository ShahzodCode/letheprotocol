# City generation, map, navigation, the war-torn city, optimisation
Read this when you touch city*.gd, world*.gd, site.gd, scenery.gd, fires.gd, veil.gd or anything about streets, buildings, climbing, fires or level of detail. Chapter One's hand-built places (Institute, home, park) are in [missions-and-story.md](missions-and-story.md).

## City generation and navigation
- `City.generate(seed)`: 5 x 5 avenues (24 m on the two through the origin, 20 m elsewhere; the start is the central junction, avenues x = 0 and z = 0 stay free, tests rely on them), blocks cut by random binary subdivision into streets (12 m), footpaths (6 m) and alleys (3 m; deeper cuts are narrower), a building on each lot: one floor (4-6.5 m), mid-rise (20-70 m), towers (80-190 m, tiered; 1 in 8 is 220-380 m with a spire), empty lots = plazas. Buildings are boxes; a shader draws windows from world coordinates (instance colour = wall colour, alpha = style). `World.height_at` is always 0 (flat).
- Navigation: `AStarGrid2D`, 1 m cells, buildings/cars solid (+0.5 m margin). Enemies wander and hunt along `City.path` (collinear points dropped), straight only when they see the player closer than 14 m.
- Spawning: `City.random_spawn` picks a street by area x class weight (`City.WEIGHTS`: avenue 1.6, plaza 0.6, street 0.5, walk 0.3, alley 0.15; the old text said avenue 1, walk 0.45, alley 0.35, OUTDATED) so avenues get most soldiers; kind and group size depend on the class (`Main.KIND_WEIGHTS`, `GROUP_SIZE`); nobody starts within 110 m of the player.
- Parked vehicles (`City.CAR_TYPES`: hatch, sedan, SUV, van, truck, bus, each with random size variation) are built from boxes in `World._cars`: paint (sometimes two-tone roof, sometimes a racing stripe), dark glass, wheels, head / tail lights; one collision box and one nav obstacle each.

## Map, start, goal, sites
- Map: outer half-extents 172 x 312 m (`City.OUT_X/OUT_Z`), playable `|x| < 150`, `|z| < 290`; the outer 22 m is a ring of contiguous tall buildings (colliders, nav-solid) - there are NO invisible walls. Through streets: x = -108 / -54 / 0 / 54 / 108 and z = -258 / -200 / -140 / -80 / 0 / 70 / 130 / 185 / 235 (widths in `City.LINE_WIDTH`); only x = 0 (24 m), z = 0 (24 m) and the Institute avenue z = 235 (20 m) are wide, the rest are 12 m streets and 8-10 m footpaths, and the blocks between them are cut further into 12 / 6 / 3 m streets (many alleys). The junction at the origin stays free (the tests use the x = 0 and z = 0 avenues). Backdrop towers beyond the ring are pictures only. The ground collider extends 300 m past the ring so tests can park enemies out there.
- Start: `City.START_POS` (-30, 0.1, 254.6) inside the Institute lobby (see "Chapter One places" below), facing north through the doorway onto the 20 m avenue z = 235. Loadout: M9 pistol (15 + 4 mags) and the arc knife only; everything else comes from dead soldiers.
- Goal: no longer a single house: the mission system (`Mission`, below) decides where the marker hangs. `World._marker` is an inverted three-sided pyramid (unshaded, `disable_fog`) 95 m up over the current objective with a faint beam; `World.set_marker_target(pos)` slides it there in 1 s (null hides it). HUD top centre: the objective text and the distance ("GET HOME - 240 m").
- Battle sites (`City.sites`, open junctions at least 120 m from the start, 70 m from the home, 50 m from the park, not the origin; `SITE_KEEP_OUT` 60 m is also tested for the Institute): `Site` nodes with craters / mud / cracks / rubble / small fires; each holds 3-4 soldiers (the first 5) (always a soldier, a marksman and a blade fighter; the first site has every kind). Soldiers roam 22 m around their spawn point. One site is forced at (0, 70) on the central avenue, the direct way home; `SITE_COUNT` is 6.
- Optimisation: `Site.lod_for(distance, current)` -> 0 hidden (> 230 m), 1 ground damage only, 2 flames + smoke + sparks, 3 also the firelight (< 55 m); Main updates it every 0.35 s, and `Site.animate` flickers only lod >= 2. Far wandering enemies (> 150 m from the player, `Enemy.LOD_DIST`) update only every 0.2 s with a scaled step. Static scenery is MultiMesh (buildings = 1, each site = 2, cars = 2, slabs / dashes / decor = 1 each).
- Border lanes (`City._make_lanes`): 8 m lanes run along the inside of the ring on all four sides, cut into sections by the through streets. Corner sections and some interior sections are filled with a building (plug); two plugs never touch, so every lane section ends at a street: avenues meet a lane in a T, you must turn, and there are no dead ends and no ring road. `City.reachable_fraction()` (flood fill over the nav grid) is tested to be ~1.
- Sites: `SITE_COUNT` 6; one is always on the central avenue (z = 70), the others come from open junctions at least 50 m apart, 120 m from the start, 70 m from the home, 50 m from the park.

## Obstacle and prop notes
- Hedgehogs (`City.obstacles`, `City._make_obstacles`, `World._obstacles`): 3 steel barricades per battle site on a circle around the fire (own random stream, junction-sized radius), the model without its snow patch, one box collider each and a blocked nav rect.

## The war-torn city (Verdance on the morning of the attack)
Everything is generated in `City.generate` (data) and drawn by `World` (geometry): `_make_cars` -> `CityWar.trees` / `wreck_cars` / `obstacles` -> `CityClimb.plan` -> `CityBuildings.dress` -> `CityClimb.build` -> `CityRoads.build` -> `_build_nav`. Small scenery goes through `Scn` (MultiMeshes, one draw call per mesh type; colliders are boxes / cylinders; `Scn.blocks` close the navigation grid; `Scn.fires` feed `Fires`). `LETHE_PLAIN=1` (environment variable) skips all of it, a baseline for frame-time comparisons.
- Time of day: about 11:30 in March: sun 53 degrees up (`rotation_degrees.x = -53`), warm white, hazy sky, grey-brown DEPTH fog (60 to 430 m), neutral grey ambient (not the blue sky) so shadows stay readable.
- Roads: through streets 10 m and wider get edge / centre (dashed on streets, double solid + lane dashes on the wide avenues) lines, stop lines and zebra crossings at every junction; manholes, drains; cracks, potholes, scorch, skids, glass and debris decals, denser near battle sites. The pavement round each building is a 0.15 m kerb (`Game.L_KERB`).
- Trees: city trees only (no scattered ones, the backdrop is all towers): every 10-14 m along the through streets 12 m and wider, 2-4 in each plaza, each in a 1 m concrete planter; park trees (hand placed) stay healthy and have no planter. `City.trees[i]["state"]`: ok / burning (orange fire-shader canopy, flames + smoke + one of 4 lights near the player) / fallen (a trunk collider 0.8 m high to jump, or propped up on its stump with 1.3 m under it to crouch under, crown collider, nav blocked) / stump. About 15 / 10 / 10 %.
- Cars (`City.cars[i]`: pos, size, yaw, `roll`, `basis`, `origin`, `rect` footprint, `dmg` = "" / burnt / burning / side / roof / crushed / doors / crashed): about 40% damaged, more near the sites; plus abandoned cars across the lanes (some rammed by a second one) and burnt vans. Burning ones have a fire entry.
- Obstacles (`CityWar.war_obstacles`): jersey barriers (also chicane lines), sandbag walls, furniture / crate / door barricades, tank traps, fallen street lamps and snapped power poles (some with sparks), rubble, shipping containers (a ladder each, `City.containers`), standing street lamps. Dense on the avenues and in rings round the sites (7-10 each), rare on the back streets, never in alleys. KEEP-CLEAR: the 7 m band along x = 0 and z = 0 (`CityWar.KEEP_CLEAR`) and the doors / junction mouths. `City.reachable_fraction()` stays about 1.
- Buildings (`CityBuildings`): `arch` apartments / shop / house / office / tower / passage, Verdance palette, per-box colour and style (`bx["color"]`, `bx["style"]`), ground-floor shop fronts + awnings + glowing signs, balconies, pitched roofs (with colliders), roof gardens, rooftop units. `damage` 0..1 per building (ranked by distance to the sites and the central avenues: top 20% heavy >= 0.66, next 40% light, rest intact; the border ring 34 / 40 / 26); heavy ones get a missing corner (`b["chunk"]`), collapsed top floors, or a hole through the facade (`b["hole"]`) with rubble in the street; 38% of the heavy ones burn. Shader: broken black windows, soot above them and from the ground, burnt-out floors, fire glow (instance custom data x = damage, y = random, z = burning). Window cells that would be smaller than a pixel fade to an average (`fwidth`, taken in uniform control flow) and the varyings are `flat`: otherwise `sin(...) * 43758` hashes shimmer.
- Moving through it (`CityClimb`): 1 in 6 flat roofs is reachable (`b["access"]`): a zig-zag fire escape (steel) or concrete stair (ramp colliders with 0.28 m steps drawn on them, landings 1.6 m, up to 11 flights) on a street side 8 m or wider, else a ladder; parapets 0.9 m with gaps; solid roof units; a door to nowhere. Ladders = `City.ladders` {pos (foot on the wall), n (away from it), top}: the player attaches within 1.1 m of the wall facing the ladder and pressing forward (or from the roof edge looking down), `Player._climb`. Bridges (`City.bridges`): two neighbouring roofs made equal in height, open footbridge (rails 0.95 m, truss) or glass walkway (roof, glass walls), some broken in the middle (a 2 m gap to jump). Crouch passages (`City.crouch_passages`): wall-to-wall blockers whose underside is 1.35 m (head crouched 1.1, standing 1.8): pipe, fallen ladder, slab, low gap; blocked in the nav grid. Pass-through buildings (`City.tunnels`): a 3 m x 3.4 m corridor across the narrow side, floor at street level, a lit ceiling strip, crates, a broken door, one pooled omni light (2 at a time), open in the nav grid (enemies path through).
- Fires: `Fires` creates flames + smoke particles only for the 16 nearest burning things within range (tree / car 85 m, building 190 m) and lights for the nearest 4 trees, 3 buildings, 2 cars, 2 corridors. Far fires are drawn as canopies / glowing windows / dark translucent smoke columns (one MultiMesh).
- Narrow streets (made denser still after the lander work, so a stealthy player always has a way round the soldiers): small blocks stop cutting with only 25% (`_subdivide`), first-level cuts are 12 m 12% / 6 m 38% / 3 m 50%, second-level 12 m 4% / 6 m 38% / 3 m 58%, and an empty lot (open square, no cover) is rare (about 3-4%, it becomes a one-floor building); open space is the 12-24 m through streets and the park. Soldiers were cut by about half (`Main.SITE_SIZE` 3-4 per battle site, the first one 5, `ROAMING_GROUPS` 4, `GROUP_SIZE` 1-3 on avenues and 1-2 / 1 elsewhere); lots are inset by up to 1.2 m, so the real opening of an alley can be wider than its street rectangle (`CityClimb._wall_dist` measures it before a blocker is built).

## Sky, landers and ambience of the invasion morning
- **Sky (`Veil`, node `World.veil`; `LETHE_NOVEIL=1` builds the old procedural sky for A/B timing).** `Veil.SKY_SHADER` is a `shader_type sky`: the old palette, a hazy sun glow, the pearly Veil (value noise on dome coordinates, an oil-on-water cosine palette that flares violently at random: `burst` = 1 of 7 time cells per second, `burst2` rarer), silent flashes (one random direction glow every 1.25 s, 55% of the cells, dies in 0.1 s) and the WOUND: the view ray is intersected with a dome `DOME_H` 480 m up, a ragged hole (two noise octaves on the radius) of `HOLE_R` 320 m over `HOLE_C` (0, 0) = the city centre (real parallax: it moves as the player walks), dark violet-red inside, a crackling blue / magenta ring on the edge. The shader does NOT read `TIME` / `POSITION` (that would make Godot re-render the sky's radiance map every frame): `t` and `cam_pos` are uniforms set from `Veil._process`; `Sky.radiance_size` is 32. 8 landers: `Veil.lander_pos(i, t)` is the analytic path (the first two already sink at t = 0, one more every 14 s, 3-4 m/s from about 440 m down to a resting height 150-325 m, then they drift outwards); drawn as ONE MultiMesh of a 56-triangle hull (lens, orange engine bell, 3 legs; unshaded vertex colours, `disable_fog`) plus ONE MultiMesh of Y-billboard quads with an additive heat-shimmer shader (no particles), both moved 4 times a second by `_update_landers` (a lander farther than 0.85 x `camera.far` is pulled in along the view line and shrunk by the same factor, so view distance 260 m still shows them at the same screen size). `PILLARS`: 5 black smoke columns (tilted tapered cylinders, one MultiMesh, unshaded, `disable_fog`) over the power stations south (z 430-470) and east (x 400-440), outside the playable ring.
- **Ambience.** `Main.avenue_closeness()` (0..1 within 45 m of the 24 m avenues x = 0 / z = 0 and the Institute avenue): the crowd loop gets a far baseline (-27 dB on the avenue even with nobody near, +5 dB with people near, fading with `Civilian.clock` over 420 s) and `_update_ambience` plays `far_gun` (8 muffled shots in 2-3 bursts) or `far_boom` (3 s deep thump + rumble) from a random direction 110-280 m away every 7 s (2.2 s on the avenue) via `Sfx.play_at(..., unit_size 70)`. Sounds are pre-built in `Main._ready`.
