## The invasion morning (veil.gd, city_story.gd, enemy.gd, main.gd, weapons_db.gd, sfx.gd; tests: `invasion_tests` in selftest.gd, `tests/story_probe.gd`)
Chapter One "IV. The Streets / V. The Road Home" made playable with the existing systems; no cutscene anywhere (objective text, two `Game.radio_line` subtitles in `ChapterOne`, title text and the environment tell it).
- **Scripted soldiers (`Main._spawn_story_enemies`, story only, before the first checkpoint; `story_roles` uid -> role, `_apply_role` re-applies it after `spawn_enemy_from`, i.e. after a checkpoint restore).** Both are alone (`group` -1) and stand at a `post` (no wandering, `post_facing`) while calm. `lane_blade`: a plain `blade` soldier (no gun: `weapon_id` "blade", no muzzle, not ranged) about 10 m inside the first narrow lane beside the walk home (`CityStory.first_lane()`: the alley / footpath in `ROUTE_ZONE` that the path from `INSTITUTE_EXIT` to `HOME_POS` passes within 26 m of, at least 35 m along it; today (12..48, 208..211), blade at (23, 209.5)); `Main._update_story` (every 0.2 s) makes him charge (`_aggravate`, `alert_t` 0.3) when the player is within `LANE_TRIGGER` 24 m with a clear line; 65 HP = 3 pistol hits, he runs 4.65 m/s. `lone_soldier`: a `soldier` (60 HP, `lazy_alert` 1 s on top of the alert reaction, `spin_rifle`: tosses and spins his rifle every 1.7 s, uses `_animate_soldier`) `LONE_BEFORE_HOME` 46 m before the home door on the walk (`CityStory.lone_soldier_spot()`, today (5.5, 7.2) on the central avenue) facing the way home, so the player coming from the Institute is behind him: silent takedown or a shot; his PR-1 is the first rifle.
- **Dressing (`CityStory`).** `dress()` runs in `City.generate` after `CityWar.obstacles` (uses `CityWar.occ`, `_free()` also avoids cars and trees): a tram on its side (16 x 3.3 x 2.6 m box, windows facing the sky, bogies, snapped pantograph with sparks, a smouldering "car"-kind fire; first free spot of `TRAM_SPOTS` in the Institute avenue, today (42, 229)), 8 overturned market stalls with spilled goods along z = 235 / 185 / 130 / 70 and x = 0 (`STALL_COUNT`). `body_spots(rng)` (needs the nav grid): a spot every `BODY_SPACING` 21 m along the walk home, against the nearest wall within 14 m (doorways) or at the street edge (gutters, 30%), spawned by `Main._populate_civilians` as `lie_down()` civilians (no shadow, not drawn beyond `Civilian.BODY_VIS` 100 m). `CIVILIAN_COUNT` 50 (9 children, was 40 / 8); `BODY_COUNT` 10 route bodies.
- **Untested by hand:** the feel of the slower invaders (3.9 m/s soldiers may feel too easy to outrun, tune `INVADER_RUN`), whether the lane blade is found at all (the lane is beside the avenue, the player may walk past), how the sky reads in motion (flicker rate, hole size), the whip-crack and far-gunfire loudness, the tram's look, and that the PR-1 viewmodel sits well in ADS.

## The Lander, part one (ship_models.gd, park_fight.gd, city_story.gd, enemy.gd, weapons.gd, missions/chapter_one.gd; tests: `lander_tests` + `lander_spawn_checks` in selftest.gd, `tests/lander_probe.gd`, `tests/sniper_view.gd`)
Chapter One "IX. The Lander" up to the run into the lanes (part two, B4: the chase, the courtyard grenades, the arm wound). After GO TO THE CELLAR (the home defence, B2, goes between it and CLIMB TO THE ROOF) the objectives are: `climb_roof` -> `lookout` (script) -> `balcony_marksman` (kill_group 201) -> `take_rifle` (script: an LR-9 in the rifle slot) -> `park_fight` (script: 3 guards dead) -> `escape_lanes` (reach). The mission ends there (`win_title` "TO BE CONTINUED"). A checkpoint starts with every one of them (the one at `park_fight` = "the start of the balcony fight": the rifle, the ten guards, the landed craft). `LETHE_PLAIN=1` has no lookout roof, so the plain story ends at the cellar.
- **The lookout roof.** `CityPlaces._old_block()`: a 4-storey (14 m) "old block" at (37, -47, 9.5 x 13) in the home's yard, flagged `lookout_plot`; `CityStory.pick_lookout()` (called first in `CityClimb._plan_access`) gives it `access` + `lookout`, `CityClimb._access` retries 16 places until the zig-zag fire escape fits on the street side (x = 54 street, `CityWar.extra_clear` keeps trees / cars / barricades off the footprint; the ladder is only a fallback), and `CityStory.lookout` = {building, side, kind, top 14, foot (Vector3 at the bottom, east face), roof (1.6 m in from the edge)}. `climb_roof` completes on the roof; `lookout` starts the landing (`Main.begin_lookout`), the marker moves to the ramp foot and the objective is done after `LOOKOUT_TIME` 6 s on the roof (radius 7 m) AND the craft has landed (nothing else: the camera is free).
- **The landing craft (`ShipModels`).** Model space: nose -z, ramp +z, ground y = 0: a grey boxy hull 10 x 6 m, 20.5 m long on four short legs, two engine pods with orange exhausts, dorsal block, cockpit band, the rear ramp lowered to the 1.5 m sill and a doorway alcove with a lit threshold line (the interior itself is NOT modelled: a dead-end alcove, B5 hooks into `Lander.entered_threshold` / `Lander.threshold`, an Area3D that sees the player). `lander_mesh(2)` (1088 triangles, panels / vents / ramp ribs / turret) and `lander_mesh(1)` (536) are cached ArrayMeshes of `GunModels.Builder` (lit + glow surface); `lander_boxes()` the collision set (body, doorway sides, header, alcove floor, 2 pods, the ramp as a rotated box); `site_mesh()` the ground patch (scorched discs and streaks, 6 crushed flowerbeds with petals, 3 knocked-over trees, planks). `City.LANDER_POS` (83, 0, -53) / `LANDER_YAW` -90 degrees: it stands on `LANDING` with the ramp facing WEST (the street side, towards the home), foot at (67, -53) (`CityStory.ramp_foot()`); `CityStory._lander_blocks()` closes the nav grid under hull and pods. `World._lander()` builds `world.lander` hidden: `begin_landing()` (9 s descent from 70 m with a hum, `touched_down` signal, dust, `far_boom`), `land_now()`, `hide_ship()`, `update_lod(distance)` every 0.35 s from `Main._update_sites` (lod 2 under 85 m, shadows under 140 m). The collider StaticBody3D has meta `hull`: a shell that hits it calls `Lander.spark()` (one reused OmniLight3D for 0.12 s + one reused CPUParticles3D). Heat shimmer = two Y-billboard quads with `Veil.SHIMMER_SHADER` above the exhausts (no particles). `Main._sync_story_world()` after a restore: index > lookout -> landed, == lookout -> descent again, < lookout -> hidden. `CityStory.far_landers` (up to 3 of the nearest plazas 90+ m from the park with a free spot found by a 2 m scan, at `FAR_SCALE` 0.8, solid box + nav block each; `World.far_landers_node` = one MultiMesh of the cheap hull) show from the lookout on (they are 190-250 m from the roof: grey blocks in the haze).
- **Exhausted soldiers (`Enemy.State.EXHAUSTED`).** Two soldiers sit against the west wall of the balcony building (`CityStory.rest_spot()`, 1.5 m apart, facing the street, role `exhausted`, group 202): `sit_down()` (legs stretched out, hips on the floor, hit boxes x 0.62, rifle across the knees, stamina pinned at `EXHAUSTED_STAMINA` 6 so the breath is the loudest), `remove_helmet()` (hides the tan mesh `Object_82` of the M4 soldier, the head gets plain skin colour). They hear half as far; `exh_notice` starts when one of them notices the player (eyes, ears or a gunshot, taken in slowly) and after `EXHAUSTED_REACT` 2 s he gets up (`_get_up`: recovering, cannot run for long, `lazy_alert` 0.7 s) and hunts; his friend within 10 m gets up a second later. `can_be_taken_down()` is true in that state from ANY side (even face to face) until he gets up; `takedown_point()` is the chest (lower while he sits). Being hit also gets him up (`_aggravate`).
- **The balcony marksman.** Role `balcony_marksman` (group 201): a `marksman` at `CityStory.MARKSMAN_AT` (77.5, 3.4, -30.7) at the north rail, `post_facing` 0 (north, the park), `sight_fov` 70 so the stair end behind him is outside his eyes; crouched, the player walks up the outside stair (`City.BALCONY_STAIR_FOOT` (66.8, 0, -30.3) -> y 3.4 at x 72) and the takedown is on offer from 2 m behind-left; two pistol hits (55 HP) also kill him; if he sees the player he charges the red laser and snipes as ever. His weapon is the LR-9 (`Main._spawn_enemy_at(pos, kind, weapon)` / `Enemy.set_weapon`, also re-applied in `spawn_enemy_from`), so `_drop_loot` drops it with 5 rounds. Two park trees that blocked the balcony's view of the ramp were moved to the east end.
- **The LR-9 long rifle (id "sniper", rifle slot, `WeaponsDB`).** Bolt-action: 120 damage (head x2.5), 40 rpm (1.5 s cycle, a bolt sound after 0.55 s), 5 rounds, 3 spare mags, reload 3.6 s, loudness 170 (heard 76 m), `ads_fov` 12 (the existing scope mask, `Weapons.is_scoped()`), ADS spread 0.02, `"heavy": true`, `"bullet_speed": 200` (slow shell: `Weapons.shell_trace()` follows `p(s) = origin + dir * s - g/2 * (s/v)^2` in 10 m pieces, so 0.2 m drop at 40 m, 0.8 m at 80 m: a first shot at a far target misses low when the player did not aim high; nothing is scripted to miss), `"scope_sway": 0.5` degrees: `Weapons._update_sway` drifts the scoped view (`Weapons.sway_view` is added to the head rotation in `Player._physics_process`), Shift or Ctrl holds the breath for `breath_time` 3 s (a tenth of the drift, a white breath bar under the scope cross, `HUD.scope_rect` uniform `breath`), then the lungs give out (`gasp_t` 1.3 s of 2.2 x drift) and 4 s refill it; movement adds drift, crouching takes 30% off. `GunModels._longrifle` (1.3 m: fluted shroud, muzzle brake with orange slots, big scope, thumbhole stock, bolt handle, folded bipod), sound `longshot` (hard crack, boom, 1.7 s tail), tracer 2 cm orange-white. Heavy: `Weapons._start_switch` drops it (a pick-up with its rounds and mags, out of the loadout, toast "DROPPED LR-9") when a hunter is within `HEAVY_DROP_RANGE` 16 m (`Weapons.hunter_near`); the pistol is the safe choice. `ParkFight` shows a toast "TOO CLOSE - SWITCH TO THE PISTOL (1)" when a hunter is within 11 m while it is in hand.
- **The park fight (`ParkFight`, node `Main.park_fight`).** Ten guards (`GUARD_KINDS`: 8 soldiers + 2 riflemen, role `park_guard`, group 200, `post` facing outwards) are spawned through Main when the craft touches down (so they are in the next checkpoint) in two rows (4.5 m and 8 m from the ramp foot) of a 75 degree fan opening west, each facing outwards so that none of them looks at the balcony (`CityStory.guard_spots()`). CALM: they see the player with their own eyes only. SEARCH: the first heard shot (`Game.gunshot` within `loudness * 0.45`) makes each of them scatter 2-5 m (`hold_order`, a 150 degree sweep) with `confused` (they see the player only inside 12 m: `_hold_step`) and registers ONE Squads record `{persist: true}` (Squads keeps it when members die and breaks it after `HOLD_TIME` 20 s). Whoever has a clear line to the muzzle, inside a 150 degree field, within 110 m catches the flash with a chance of 1 in 2 per shot: after `CHARGE_DELAY` 1 s the circle breaks (`Squads._break`: to cover, then CHASE). CHARGE also starts after the 20 s or when a guard finds the player on his own; for `TRACK_TIME` 14 s their chase keeps `last_known` = the player (the flash gave the balcony away). Radio subtitles: "SNIPER! GET DOWN!" ... "THE BALCONY! HE IS ON THE BALCONY!". `park_fight` is done after 3 dead guards (`KILLS_TO_CONTINUE`), then `escape_lanes` (`CityStory.escape_lane()`: an alley / footpath of at most 6.5 m, 14-80 m long, 30-90 m from the balcony, south of its building, reachable). `ParkFight.reset()` (after a restore) puts the controller back to CALM.
- **Untested by hand:** whether the three far craft can be seen at all from the roof (towers hide most of them: the sinking Veil landers in the sky are what the lookout sees for sure), how the lookout feels (6 s, the camera is free), whether the exhausted pair's squat reads as sitting, the marksman approach in real play (the stair is 5 m long and crouch-walking is slow), the LR-9's feel (sway amount, breath bar, drop at 40 m, recoil, bolt sound), the guards' 20 s timing, whether three kills are enough for "thin out", the dust and spark effects, and the whole run to the lanes at speed.

## The Lander, part two (chase_fight.gd, squads.gd, enemy.gd, weapons.gd, player.gd, hud.gd, city_places.gd, city_story.gd, missions/chapter_one.gd; tests: `chase_start_checks` + `chase_tests` in selftest.gd, `tests/chase_run.gd`, `tests/chase_probe.gd`)
Chapter One "IX. The Lander", second half. After THIN OUT THE GUARDS the objectives are: `escape_lanes` (reach the mouth of the west lane of the neighbourhood) -> `take_grenades` (script: 3 grenades in the pocket) -> `clear_courtyard` (script: done by the wound) -> `finish_them` (script: the courtyard squad is dead) -> `ramp` (reach the lander's open ramp, radius 3.5, then the slice ends: "TO BE CONTINUED"). A checkpoint starts with each (the hook runs first: the chase, the gathering, the spare grenade are all in it).
- **Noah's neighbourhood (`CityPlaces.court_block`, `City.COURT_BLOCK` (60, 12, 43 x 52) = the block south of the cross avenue under the park).** Hand-built from story buildings (no random damage / roof access / passages): a west block 11.6 m, a NORTH WING 23 x 18 m with a flat 4.6 m roof and no parapet (`City.COURT_ROOF_Y`), a tall south wing (8.6 m), low shops (5.2 m) on the south street, an east strip (5.5 m), four gate piers; between them the 3 m alleys AW (x 70..73, z 12..64), AE (x 96..99) and a cross alley (z 54..57) and the COURTYARD `City.COURT_RECT` (78, 30, 12 x 12; centre `City.COURT_POS` (84, 0, 36)) with two 3 m gates (west x 73..78, east x 90..96 at z 34.5..37.5, `CityPlaces.court_gates`), paving, two crates, a warm light. The street entries carry `"open": true` (no crouch passage in them). Four drainpipes (`CityPlaces.pipes`, built by `build_pipes()` after `CityClimb.build`, which clears the ladder list): `City.ladders` entries of kind "pipe" (a 16 cm pipe with brackets drawn through `Scn`; the player attaches, climbs at 2.5 m/s, steps onto the roof): north wing west face z 20 (the way up, `CityStory.pipe_foot()`), north wing east face z 25, the south shops z 60.5, the east strip z 40. No battle site within 75 m of `COURT_POS`. Empty lots (plazas) are now rare (`City._wants_square`: big lots only, at most `MAX_SQUARES` 4; the far landers use them). `CityStory.escape_lane()` is now the mouth of AW (71.5, 16), 54 m by road from the balcony stair foot.
- **The chase (`ChaseFight.begin()`, on_start of `escape_lanes`).** Six of the living guards (nearest to the player first; surplus guards leave the world; fewer than six are made up at the ramp) get the roles "chaser" (4) and "straggler" (2: the two soldiers farthest from the player) and hunt (`state` CHASE, `last_known` = the player), `group` -1 (Squads' generic regrouping ignores them), `ParkFight.dismiss()`. `Enemy` got: `sight_scale` (multiplies sight, eyes and the seen test), `seen_delay` (a hunting soldier needs the player in view this long before `seen_now`), `pace` (multiplies chase speed), `fatigue` (multiplies the stamina drain of running), `damage_scale`, `grenade_drop` (exactly N grenades, with `Pickup.keep` = they never fade), `straggler` (a WINDED straggler can be knifed from behind), `hold_walk_max` (HOLD phase 0 time limit). Every 0.25 s `_tick_chase`: a chaser in a 3 m alley (`is_narrow`: street class alley, at most 4.5 m wide) gets `sight_scale` 0.55 and `seen_delay` 0.7 s (open street: 1.0 / 0.15); bunching: out of sight of the player, in an alley, within 3.5 m of a bend of his nav path and with a man 7..30 m behind him, he stops 1.4 s (`pace` 0), then 4.5 s of no waiting; splitting: two or more who lost him for 1.2 s each get a different search point (`last_known`, 12..36 m away, greedy farthest from the others, 12 s cooldown) with the line "SPLIT UP! CHECK THE ALLEYS!". Stragglers: stamina starts at half, drains 2.4 times faster (winded after about 1.2 s of running), drop 2 grenades each. Guaranteed grenades: 2 on the low roof (`CityStory.court_stash()`, `keep`) + 2 per straggler = 6 (the player carries 4 at most); `take_grenades` completes with 3 in the pocket (`GRENADES_NEEDED`); its marker follows the nearest straggler, else the nearest grenade on the ground.
- **The courtyard (`ChaseFight.gather()`, on_start of `clear_courtyard`, also by itself after 30 s of the chase once all chasers lost him).** The four non-stragglers (made up to four at the ring if the player killed some) become "courtyard" (the last two "courtyard_last": `damage_scale` 0.4, so one grenade rarely kills them) and `Squads.form_back_to_back(members, centre)`: a record of kind "b2b" (persistent, no 20 s limit, not broken by deaths or by a grenade landing nearby), radius 2.2 m, each member `hold_order`ed to his place with `hold_walk_max` 60 s, facing outwards, a sector of TAU / n; HOLD never chases, shoots on sight (a head over the roof edge is seen when the ray from the courtyard floor clears the 4.6 m lip: standing 1 m back from the edge is seen, 5 m back is not), radio lines "Watch the roofs!" ... The player climbs the pipe, throws grenades (`Grenade.predict` preview; the grenades bounce off the courtyard walls, blast radius 7 m, only with a clear ray).
- **The wound (`ChaseFight._wound`).** With two or fewer courtyard men alive (`WOUND_MAX_ALIVE`) they are wounded (health at most 28, `damage_scale` 1) and the next bolt one of them fires (`Game.enemy_fired`, emitted by `Enemy._fire_bolt` at the player) within 45 m wounds the arm; a wounded man with no line to the roof for 2.5 s (the blast threw him under the lip) drags himself to the middle; if the whole circle dies before anybody shot, the dying burst wounds the arm anyway. `Weapons.wound_arm()`: `Game.arm_injured` = true, every rifle falls to the ground as a pick-up (the loadout's rifle slot is emptied), the pistol comes up, 12 damage (never lethal), shake, `Game.arm_wounded`, toast "RIGHT ARM HIT - THE RIFLE FALLS". Effects for the rest of the chapter (all keyed on `Game.arm_injured`): `Weapons.slot_locked()` (rifle and melee slots: the keys and the cycle skip them, a pick-up is refused, toasts "CAN'T HOLD A RIFLE" / "CAN'T USE THE KNIFE", no takedown), spread x `WOUND_SPREAD_MULT` 2 + 1.2 degrees, bloom x 1.8, reload x 1.7 (`reload_total()`, also the HUD bar), walking x `WOUND_MOVE` 0.93, the viewmodel mirrored to the left of the view (position x and yaw / roll negated) and the throwing arm on the left, a limp bloody sleeve (`wound_arm_node`) at the lower right, HUD `_ArmIcon` on the health plate, and `Player._drip`: a dark red spot every 1.4 m walked (or every 3 s standing), a ring of `DRIP_MAX` 12 shared-mesh quads reused oldest first. `Checkpoint.arm_injured` is in the snapshot (restore also clears the drips); a restore before the wound gives the rifle back (it is in the saved loadout).
- **FINISH THEM (`on_finish_start`, phase FINISH).** A grenade is guaranteed (a `keep` pick-up at the player's feet if the pocket is empty). `Game.exploded` within `FINISH_RADIUS` 11 m of the courtyard centre: after 0.6 s whoever is left there is killed (scripted); with nobody left `finish_them` completes at once. Then `ramp`.
- **Roles after a restore:** `Main._apply_role` handles chaser / straggler / courtyard / courtyard_last first (no post; straggler flags; `damage_scale`); `ChaseFight.sync()` (from `Main._sync_story_world`) re-hunts the chasers (escape_lanes / take_grenades), re-forms the b2b squad (clear_courtyard / finish_them) or does nothing (ramp).
- **Also changed with this task:** the soldiers were halved (battle sites 3-4, roaming groups 4) and the blocks cut into more narrow streets (see "Narrow streets"); the first-lane blade fighter must stand 65 m or more from the start; the lone soldier's spot steps aside off the avenue's clear band.
- **Untested by hand:** the feel of the chase (are 3.9 m/s chasers ever a threat to a sprinting player? the alley sight / delay numbers), whether the bunching and splitting read in play, the courtyard roof edge (how close to the lip is safe), how hard the grenade throws are from the roof with the arc preview, whether the wound is dramatic enough (12 damage, shake, toast), the left-hand viewmodel and the limp-arm sleeve in the corner of the screen, the blood drip look, and the whole run at speed.
