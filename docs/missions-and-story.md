# Missions, checkpoints, Chapter One places, story status
Read this when you touch mission.gd, checkpoint.gd, missions/chapter_one.gd, city_places.gd or the hand-built places. The long design notes of the three story chunks are in [story-chapter-one-details.md](story-chapter-one-details.md).

## The game and the story
First-person shooter prototype in **Godot 4.x (GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer; developed with 4.7.2, `project.godot` says 4.7)**. Lore: one man fights a war against an army. Chapter One: Noah, a teenager, survives the invasion of Verdance, Earth's capital (a modern city built inside a restored forest). Current slice: you start in the lobby of the Institute (the tallest, pale tower of the district) at the south end of a city strip (about 300 x 580 m) with only a pistol and a knife, walk about 280 m to your family's apartment (a glowing triangle marks the current objective) and go down to its cellar. A park with a balcony building (a later landing ship fight) lies near the home. Soldiers patrol the streets (wide avenues the most); some stand in big groups at burnt-out battle sites. A ring of tall buildings closes the map. Successor of the Bot Shooter prototype (Three.js, repo `game`); movement, weapon and AI rules were carried over from it. Claude is in charge of the project; the user directs it step by step.

The chapter documents are in the folder `The Story/` (they stay there; not read by default): `Chapter One The Morning the Veil Broke.docx` (Chapter One, played in the current slice: the invasion morning, the walk home, the cellar, the lander), `Chapter Two The Pit.docx` (not implemented), `The Long Reach A History of the Colonial Age.docx` (background history). The current objective list is in `scripts/missions/chapter_one.gd`.

## Missions, checkpoints
- `Mission` (scripts/mission.gd) is an ordered list of objectives made with `Mission.obj(id, text, opts)`: `type` reach (within `radius`, default 7 m, on the ground, optional `vertical` limit) / kill_group (every enemy with `group` dead) / interact (E within 2 m of `pos`, HUD shows "E - label") / script (only `Mission.complete(id)`); optional `pos`, `marker_pos`, `on_start` / `on_complete` Callables. Completing one shows "OBJECTIVE COMPLETE", plays the `chime` and starts the next; the last one sets `Game.won`. Signals on `Game`: `objective_changed(id)`, `objective_completed(id, text)`. `Mission.respawn_enemies` is false for the story (`ChapterOne.build()`), true for `Mission.sandbox()` (`-- --sandbox`).
- Checkpoints (`Checkpoint`): `Main` captures one every time an objective starts (after `on_start`). It holds player place / view / health, the whole `Weapons.loadout` (loaded rounds and every spare magazine), `Game.kills` and the shot counters, the living enemies (uid, kind, group, place, health, ammo) and the uids of the dead, and the loot on the ground. Enemies carry a `uid` from `Main._spawn_enemy_at`. The death screen: R = `Game.checkpoint_requested` -> `Checkpoint.restore` (frees every enemy node and pickup, rebuilds the living ones calm, restores loadout and counters, teleports the player and waits two physics frames), Shift+R = reload the scene (whole mission). Hooks that spawn enemies must go through `Main._spawn_enemy_at` so they get a uid.

## Chapter One places (city.gd + city_places.gd, plan view: x east, z south)
- Institute: tower on the old start block (footprint x -42..-18, z 245..269), pale stone, ~182 m (`style` 0.5, lit crown). Lobby `City.LOBBY` 16 x 12 x 5 m, 3 m doorway in the front wall (z = 245) onto the avenue, reception desk + two pillars (props), glowing lift door (decor, unusable), one omni light. `INSTITUTE_POS`, `START_POS`, `INSTITUTE_EXIT` (on the avenue, first objective).
- Home: block x 12..48, z -74..-12, east of the central avenue near the cross avenue; a 6 m footpath at z -59..-53 splits off the north lots. 4-storey block (13.2 m), only the ground apartment (14 x 10 m inside, `HOME_ORIGIN`, 3 m ceilings, walls 0.2 m, doorways 1.2 m wide, no door leaves): front door on the west side (`HOME_POS`), hallway, kitchen with counter (cover), living room, bedroom, bathroom, and a hallway door to the stairwell. Stairs (smooth 39 degree ramp collider with visual steps) go down to the cellar (`CELLAR_RECT` 6 x 5 m, floor y -2.7, ceiling y -0.3 = 2.4 m clear, shelves along the walls) under the yard. `CELLAR_DOOR_POS` (top of the stairs), `CELLAR_POS` (cellar centre, y -2.7).
- Underground decision: the ground collider has three layers (top -0.3..0 open over `STAIR_HOLE`, middle -2.7..-0.3 open over the stair hole and the cellar, solid bottom); the ground mesh and every slab are cut around the stair hole; liners (decor boxes) draw the cellar walls, floor and ceiling. Navigation stays flat per grid: `City.layers` are finer AStarGrid2D grids (apartment 0.25 m cells, cellar 0.25 m, balcony 0.5 m) with a parent grid, a link point (`outer`), `via` waypoints and an entry point (`inner`); `City.path()` joins the pieces (main -> doorstep -> hallway -> stair top -> down the ramp -> cellar), `layer_of(pos)` picks the grid by position AND height. Enemies follow those waypoints with their normal physics, so they walk down the ramp. `reachable_fraction()` only looks at the main grid.
- Distance and routes: the home is 250-300 m from the Institute doorway by road (tested). Direct route: the 24 m avenue x = 0; with that avenue closed a longer route through x = +-54 / z = 185 streets, footpaths and alleys still exists (tested). `City.spawn_weight()` scales street weights inside `ROUTE_ZONE` (alleys / footpaths x 0.25, avenue x 1.6).
- Park: block x 60..103, z -74..-12 (`PARK_RECT` 40 x 40, `PARK_POS`), grass, paths, pond (flat blue prop), benches, 12 trees; `LANDING` 30 x 15 m in the middle stays clear (tested). On its south side a 3-storey building with an open second-floor balcony (`BALCONY_RECT`, `BALCONY_POS`, y 3.4, railing on the park side) reached by an outside ramp stair at its west end (a nav layer linked at the stair foot).
- Trees: see "The war-torn city": city trees in planters along the through streets and in plazas (some burning, fallen or burnt), the 12 park trees are healthy; the backdrop beyond the ring is all towers (`City.far_trees` is empty).
- Data helpers: `City.props` (furniture / partitions with a collider; `nav` names the grid they block), `City.ramps`, `City.lights`, interior walls use window-shader style 0.99 (plain) and decor "liners" cover the inside of exterior walls. Slabs carry a `lift` (0 footpaths, 1 yards, 2 floors / paths, 3 tiles; 1 cm each) to avoid z-fighting.
