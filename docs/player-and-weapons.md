# Player, controls, weapons, loadout, grenades
Read this when you touch player.gd, weapons.gd, weapons_db.gd, gun_models.gd, pickup.gd, grenade.gd or the HUD ammo / pickup parts. Enemy-side grenade and squad rules are in [enemies-and-ai.md](enemies-and-ai.md).

## Controls and player
- Controls: WASD, Space jump (hold = higher), Shift sprint (forward only, cancels ADS), Ctrl crouch (Shift-sprint then Ctrl = slide), W into a ladder = climb (W / S up / down at 2.5 m/s, Space = jump off backwards, no firing but weapons can be switched, the top steps onto the roof), LMB fire, RMB aim, R reload, 1 / 2 / 3 slot (press again = swap with the slot's other weapon), Tab / wheel cycle, Q pick up, E interact (interact objectives) or silent TAKEDOWN, hold G = grenade arc preview and release throws (tap = throw at once), Esc pause (click to resume). Death screen: R = restart from the last checkpoint, Shift+R = restart the whole mission.
- Input map (checked against game_state.gd): move_forward/back/left/right = W S A D, jump = Space, sprint = Shift, crouch = Ctrl, reload = R, slot_1/2/3 = 1 2 3, pickup = Q, interact = E, grenade = G, cycle_weapon = Tab, restart = Repl (death screen), pause = Esc, debug_overlay = F3, fire = LMB, aim = RMB, wheel up / down. The mouse sensitivity constant is in game_state.gd (0.0022 rad/px according to the old status text, unverified here).
- Player (`player.gd`, values in `Game`): 100 HP, no regeneration. Walk 5, sprint 8, crouch 2.4 m/s; jump 7 m/s (variable height), gravity 24 (x1.25 falling), coyote 0.12 s, jump buffer 0.12 s, step-up over ledges <= 0.55 m (camera eases), uphill slower / downhill faster, slides on slopes > 50 degrees, fall damage above 15 m/s impact (5.5 per m/s, no cap: 2 storeys ~29, 4 storeys ~75, 6 storeys ~110 = deadly; a pain grunt, a stronger landing dip and shake), crouch-slide 0.85 s, footsteps, landing dip. Solid against enemies.

## Weapons
- Weapons (`weapons_db.gd`): PISTOL (semi-auto 420 rpm, 28 dmg, head x2.5, 15 round mag, 4 spare, 1.6 s reload) and RIFLE (auto 720 rpm, 24 dmg, head x2.2, 30 rounds, 4 spare, 2.5 s reload). Hitscan; direction = camera forward + random cone: hip / ADS spread + movement + airborne + bloom per shot (crouching x0.75); damage falls off with distance (full to `falloff_start`, `falloff_min` at `falloff_end`); legs do 75%. Recoil climbs the view itself (pitch + random yaw) and settles back (`recoil_recover`), ADS x0.7. Ammo is magazine based: reloading throws away the rounds left in the gun (no tactical reload) and loads the fullest spare magazine; the HUD shows only the NUMBER of spare magazines; empty trigger pull clicks and starts the reload. Shots hit trees / rocks / terrain (impact sparks), hits push enemies (`impulse`), tracers, ejected brass (RigidBody3D, max 40 alive), muzzle flash light + glow quad, sway / bob / reload / switch animations. Gunshots (`Game.gunshot`) alert wanderers within `loudness * 0.45` m.
- The weapons list is now pistol, rifle (PR-1), carbine (PC-24), hmg (HG-60), railgun (RG-5), sniper (LR-9), knife, blade (`WeaponsDB.ORDER`).
- HUD: top-left kills, bottom-left health blocks, bottom-right ammo box (loaded / reserve / mags / reload bar), crosshair gaps open with the real spread (a red dot while aiming), hit marker (white hit, yellow headshot, red kill), red vignette when hurt, death screen with accuracy, pause screen, a small context prompt under the crosshair (`HUD._Context`: "CTRL CROUCH" with a crouching figure before a crouch passage, "W CLIMB" at a ladder).

## Loadout, loot, ammo
- Player carries at most 2 weapons per slot: pistol, rifle (any rifle-class gun: PR-1 pulse rifle, pulse carbine, heavy pulse gun, rail rifle, LR-9 long rifle), melee (arc knife, plasma blade). Starts with M9, knife (the first rifle comes from a dead soldier: the PR-1 pulse rifle, formerly the M4). `Weapons.loadout[slot]` = list of instances `{id, ammo, spare: Array[int]}`; every instance owns its rounds and finite spare mags (max `MAX_SPARE_MAGS` 6). `Weapons.state` is `{current id: active instance}` (kept for the tests).
- Keys: 1 / 2 / 3 slot (again = other weapon in the slot), Tab / wheel cycle, Q pick up (cycle moved from Q to Tab). Melee: LMB swings (`reach`, `damage` in the DB; hits the nearest enemy in a 44 degree cone with a clear ray, mid-swing).
- Dead enemies drop (`Enemy._drop_loot`): their weapon with the rounds still in it, 2 loose magazines of that type, and (soldier / rifleman, 50 %) an M9 sidearm. Enemies have infinite magazines but a real magazine each (`mag_size` from the DB, they reload when dry: `reloading`, DB `reload_time`).
- Standing within 2 m of a lying weapon shows the pick-up box on the right of the screen (`Weapons.hover`); Q takes it with its remaining rounds and a FULL set of spare mags. Full slot: it replaces the weapon in hand (or last used in that slot) and that one is dropped with its rounds and mags. Loose magazines are collected automatically within 1.8 m, but only for a weapon you carry that has room.
- HUD: magazine strip (loaded + each spare mag filled by its rounds), slot strip above the ammo box, pick-up box, toast.

## Pickups, sights, scopes
- `Pickup` expires: weapons after 90 s, loose magazines after 60 s, blinking for the last 8 s (`LIFE_WEAPON`, `LIFE_AMMO`, `WARN_TIME`).
- Guns without a .glb (everything but the M9) have iron sights (`SIGHT_H`); `Weapons._ads_pos` places them so the sight tips sit on the line of sight (their `ads_pos` entry in the DB is unused). Weapons with `ads_fov < 40` (rail rifle) hide the viewmodel when fully aimed and the HUD draws a scope mask (`Weapons.is_scoped()`, `HUD.scope_rect` shader).

## Weapon models
- `GunModels.build(id, lod)` returns a MeshInstance3D sharing a cached mesh (lit surface + unshaded glow surface, vertex colours, no culling). lod 2 = viewmodel (rings, vents, trigger, grip wraps...), lod 1 = world (soldiers' hands, pickups). `GunModels.profile(id)` gives the side-view rectangles the HUD pick-up box draws; `grip(id)` / `muzzle(id)` position the hand and the bolt origin on a soldier.
- Guns: PC-24 carbine (cyan coils + cell), HG-60 heavy gun (shroud with vents, ammo drum, heat-fin lights), RG-5 rail rifle (twin rails with plasma core and coil housings, scope, skeletal stock, battery), M9 procedural version for soldiers and pickups, PR-1 pulse rifle (see "The invasion morning"). Melee: arc knife (steel blade, glowing edge, grip rings), plasma blade (emitter housing, crossguard, plasma blade with white core). The viewmodel melee weapons are tilted tip-up (`Weapons._build_model`).
- `center_z` of the carbine / heavy gun / rail rifle in the DB was moved forward (-0.14 / -0.2 / -0.26) so their rear end is not under the camera when firing from the hip; `Weapons._ads_pos` adapts.
- Models: the M9 viewmodel is fitted at load from the .glb bounds (`models/rifle.glb`, the old M4, is no longer used) (`Weapons._build_model`: `rot_y`, `length`, `top_y`, `center_z`; `mirror_z` adds a mirrored twin for half-models like the pistol) and their materials are matted down by `_tone_materials`. Every other weapon is procedural: add a `_myweapon(b, lod, glow, length)` to `gun_models.gd` (barrel along -z, receiver top at y = 0, centred on z = 0, nothing above the iron sights, parts at `lod >= 2` are viewmodel-only detail), a `grip()` and `muzzle()` entry, a `WEAPONS` entry (+ `glow`, `sound`), the id in `ORDER`, a sound in `sfx.gd`. Soldiers, pickups and HUD icons then use it automatically.
- PC-24 carbine (rifleman's gun) is a pale bullpup like the agile soldier's own rifle; the PR-1 (formerly the M4) is a long slim pulse rifle: bone shroud over a graphite receiver, white glowing flank channels, emitter ring, skeletal stock.
- **PR-1 pulse rifle (the old M4, id stays "rifle", stats unchanged).** `WeaponsDB`: name "PR-1 PULSE RIFLE", `model` "" (procedural `GunModels._rifle`: bone shroud, graphite receiver, white flank channels, emitter ring, skeletal stock, rust plates), `glow` white-blue (the tracer colour, 12 mm wide via `tracer_w`), `sound` "whip" (`Sfx`: no bang, a sharp whip-crack with a thin falling zip, 0.32 s), `casing` false; `Sfx` "hum" (1 s whole-cycle loop) plays from `Weapons.hum` while it is in hand. Soldiers' bolts (`KINDS.soldier.bolt`) and shot sound are the same, so every soldier cracks like it.

## Grenades (player side)
- Player: up to `Game.MAX_GRENADES` 4 (`Game.grenades`, story starts with 0, checkpoints keep it), HUD badge left of the ammo box. Hold G = dotted arc + ring (`Weapons._show_preview`, `Grenade.predict`, the same `sim_step` as the real thing), release throws, a tap (under 0.18 s) throws at once; 16 m/s along the aim plus the player's velocity; the weapon lowers and a sleeve + grenade arm swings. `Weapons.throw_grenade()` is the code path.
- `Grenade`: frozen RigidBody3D set before `add_child`, moved by `sim_step` (gravity 18, bounce 0.4, friction 0.6, rest below 1.2 m/s), fuse 2.5 s, blinking red lamp + light, emits `Game.grenade_landed` on the first floor contact. `detonate()`: radius 7 m, 140 at the centre to 0 at the edge (`damage_at`), only with a clear world ray from the blast (walls protect), enemies are pushed, the player is hurt too, `Player.add_shake` (full within 10 m, none beyond 30 m), flash light 0.15 s, fireball quad, smoke, debris, scorch quad (60 s, max 12), `explosion` sound, `Game.exploded` and `Game.gunshot(pos, 140)` (enemies within 63 m go to the blast point). Enemies do NOT throw grenades yet.
- Loot: soldiers and riflemen drop a grenade pickup 35% of the time, gunners 60% (`"grenade"` in KINDS); `Pickup.is_grenade`, collected within 1.8 m while below the limit ("+1 GRENADE" toast), expires like magazines.
