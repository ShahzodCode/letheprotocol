# Enemies, AI, stealth, squads, characters
Read this when you touch enemy.gd, squads.gd, char_model.gd, park_fight.gd / chase_fight.gd behaviour or anything soldiers see, hear, hunt or do. Tuned numbers: `Enemy.KINDS` is the only place. Civilians are in [civilians.md](civilians.md); story-specific scripted soldiers in [story-chapter-one-details.md](story-chapter-one-details.md).

## Enemy kinds (enemy.gd `KINDS`, tuned there only)
The HP and damage numbers below were corrected against `KINDS` (the old text disagreed with its own "Strength" bullet).
- soldier: 90 HP (old text: 100), PR-1 pulse rifle (4-round bursts of 5 dmg (old text: 6), strafes, holds ~18 m) and a rifle-butt (13, old text: 16) when you are in his face. blade: 65 HP (old text: 70), fast (6.2 m/s), plasma blade, melee 18 (old text: 22), reach 2.4.
- rifleman: 70 HP (old text: 80), holds ~22 m, strafes, 3-round bursts of 5.5 dmg (old text: 7). gunner: 190 HP (old text: 220), slow, 8-round bursts of 4 dmg (old text: 5).
- marksman: 55 HP (old text: 60), sees 85 m, red laser charges 1.8 s (old text: 1.5) and LOCKS at 65% (unverified): a shot at the locked point (30 dmg, old text: 38) misses if the player moved; a hit interrupts the charge.
- Ranged units fire hitscan with a visible glowing bolt (spread grows with player speed); no `Game.gunshot`, so they don't wake others. (The old sentence "Spotting the player alerts squadmates within 20 m" is OUTDATED: see Group alerts below, three must notice first.)
- Spawn with `Enemy.setup(player, pos, kind)` then `add_child`. Visor/weapon glow colour identifies the kind (green soldier, violet blade, cyan rifleman, orange gunner, red marksman).

## Strength, group alerts
- Group alerts (`Enemy._alert_group`): every enemy has a `group` (campfire index, or 100 + n for roaming groups). Nothing is shared between soldiers until THREE of the same group have noticed the player with their own senses (`noticed_self`: eyes, ears, being shot, hearing a gunshot); then the rest of the group is told. So a sniper that spots you from afar shoots alone. The old "friends within 22 m hear the commotion" and "squad within 20 m" alerts are gone.
- Strength (HP / damage per hit): soldier 90 / 5 (club 13), rifleman 70 / 5.5, gunner 190 / 4, marksman 55 / 30 (1.8 s laser), blade 65 / 18; longer pauses between bursts and wider spread than before; alert reaction 0.6 s.

## Soldier senses and hunt
- Senses (`Enemy._notices_player`): eyes = front cone (`vision_fov()`, 25% wider while SUSPICIOUS) up to `sight` (x0.6 if the player is crouched) with a clear line; ears = any direction: sprinting 20 m, walking 11 m, standing still 6 m (all halved while winded), CROUCHED = silent (only bumping into him, 1 m, counts); through a wall only half range and not if crouched. So a crouched player can walk up behind a soldier unnoticed; standing up next to him gets him heard.
- Hunt: ALERT -> CHASE to `last_known` (updated while he has a clear line to the player within `sight * 1.6`) -> SHOOT when in `engage` range with a line. Unseen for `LOSE_TIME` (4 s) -> `_give_up_hunt()`: wander from where he stands. SHOOT drops back to CHASE after 1 s without a line.
- Enemy speeds (see also the invaders bullet below): `KINDS` holds the pre-invasion numbers (run soldier 5.2, rifleman 5.0, blade 6.2, marksman 4.7, gunner 3.2); `Enemy.setup` multiplies run by `INVADER_RUN` 0.75 and walk by `INVADER_WALK` 0.9 (Earth gravity tires them): real run speeds soldier 3.9, rifleman 3.75, blade 4.65, marksman 3.5, gunner 2.4 m/s; the player sprints at 8 and outruns all of them.

## Invaders (the invasion-morning look)
- **Invaders.** `Enemy.TINT` / `RUST`: every kind is multiplied rust-coloured (agile / marksman bases are dark, so their tints exceed 1.0); the blade fighter's violet is his plasma blade (`WeaponsDB` glow). Speeds x `INVADER_RUN` 0.75 / `INVADER_WALK` 0.9 (see "Enemy speeds"), run clip played slower with a bigger forward hunch above `RUN_ANIM_SPEED` 2.2, `STAMINA_DRAIN_RUN` 18, `BREATH_RANGE` 14 and a louder pant. Winded next to a wall they lean on it (see Stamina). They stay dangerous in groups (the three-to-alert rule, squads) and weak alone.

## Stealth (enemy.gd, weapons.gd, hud.gd)
- Silent takedown: with a melee weapon in hand (`Weapons.find_takedown`), an enemy in reach and in front of the blade who is WANDER or SUSPICIOUS, has not `noticed_self` and has his back turned (player more than 120 degrees off his facing) dies instantly and silently (`Enemy.takedown()` = `_die(..., silent)`, no death cry, no alert, NO `Game.gunshot`). E or LMB does it; the HUD shows "E - TAKEDOWN" (`Weapons.takedown_target`); a paired grab-and-stab animation (see Takedown pair under Characters) + `stab` sound; counted in `Game.takedowns` (end screens, checkpoints). Melee on anyone else is the normal swing / damage. The takedown needs a melee weapon in hand (E does nothing otherwise).
- Bodies stay: `Enemy.bodies` (cap `BODY_CAP` 60, oldest freed first). A settled body stops its physics. Checkpoint restore removes them all.
- SUSPICIOUS state: a WANDER (or already suspicious) enemy who sees a body he has not seen before (inside `sight_range`, in his cone, clear line; `seen_bodies` remembers it, once per enemy) walks to it (phase 0), looks around `SUSPECT_LOOK_TIME` 8 s (1), searches points within 8 m for `SUSPECT_SEARCH_TIME` 15 s (2), then wanders again from where he stands. His cone is 25% wider (`vision_fov()`). It is NOT an alert: `noticed_self` stays false (the three-to-alert group rule ignores suspicious soldiers) until he sees / hears the player, is shot or hears a gunshot (`_aggravate`). `notice_progress()` (0..1, nearer = closer to noticing) feeds the HUD eye.
- Stamina (`"stamina"` in KINDS, default 100, gunner 70, blade 130): running flat out drains 18/s (`STAMINA_DRAIN_RUN`: a soldier is winded after about 5.5 s, the blade fighter after 7 s), walking regains 8/s, standing 20/s. At 0 he is WINDED for 3-5 s: stops, bends forward at the hips (animation), cannot shoot (`_shoot_logic`), hearing range halves, regains 6/s; afterwards (`recovering`) he moves at 60% speed until stamina > 40 (slow jog regains 4/s). A winded soldier with a wall within 1.8 m (`_find_lean_wall`, 4 rays once) shuffles to it, turns to it and leans on it (`lean_wall`, `lean_dist`, bend -1.0 rad); in the open he just stands bent over.
- Breathing: `Enemy.breath_player` (AudioStreamPlayer3D, looped synthesised `breath`) plays only while the player is within `BREATH_RANGE` 14 m; pitch (rhythm) and volume rise as stamina drops, loudest when winded.
- HUD `_Stealth`: nothing when hidden; yellow eye that fills with `notice_progress()` of the nearest suspicious enemy; red eye when anyone hunts (ALERT / CHASE / ATTACK / SHOOT / HOLD within 120 m); short yellow / red arcs round the crosshair toward suspicious or shooting enemies.

## Squads
- Squads (`Squads`, node in Main): a group (`Enemy.group`) that became alerted with N members and has lost at least 40% of N with at least 3 survivors regroups: rally point near the middle of the survivors, open and 3 m from walls (`City.is_free` ring); each survivor gets `hold_order(spot, face, sector)` (state HOLD: phase 0 run to his place on the circle, 1 hold: sweep his sector, shoot at the player on sight, never chase). Breaks on a grenade landing within 8 m of the rally point, a member dying, or after `HOLD_TIME` 20 s: `hold_scatter(cover)` (nearest point out of the player's sight) then CHASE. Radio subtitles (`Game.radio_line`, HUD bottom, `radio` crackle) while the player is within 25 m of the rally point.

## Characters (char_model.gd, enemy.gd, civilian.gd)
- `CharModel.make(id, height, outfit)`: `people` (FBX, one shared skeleton + ~11 outfits as separate meshes: the unwanted outfits and props are removed, ~1.86 m natural height), `marksman` (Mixamo rig, Idle / Walk / Run / Rifle_stand / Rifle_run / Rifle_crouch / Crouch), `agile` (futuristic armour, 18 clips: walk, run, strafes, shooting, reload, melee, death...), `soldier` (M4 soldier). All imported models face +z; `pivot` turns them to -z. Heights are fitted from per-model constants in `DEFS` (`height` in the model's own units, `foot` = floor offset). The clips are not looped in the files: `DEFS[..]["loop"]` lists the logical names that are set to loop. Logical names: idle, walk, run, aim, shoot, run_shoot, strafe_l / r, back, crouch, crouch_walk, reload, melee, death, hit.
- Kind -> model (`Enemy.MODEL_OF`): soldier -> soldier, rifleman + blade -> agile (every kind is tinted dull rust by `Enemy.TINT` / `RUST`; the blade fighter keeps his violet plasma blade through the weapon glow), marksman + gunner -> marksman (gunner tinted, 1.18x). `Enemy._animate_clips` picks the clip from the state (strafe / run-and-shoot / reload / melee / aim ...); the marksman crouches (`Rifle_crouch`) while he aims (his bone hit boxes drop with the clip).
- The soldier's weapon is OUR weapon model (`GunModels`, the same mesh as the pick-up), never the one built into the model (those are hidden: `DEFS[..]["hide"]`). Clip models hold it in the right hand: `CharModel.hand_fit()` works out the grip transform from the hidden built-in gun and caches it per model; `hold(node)` attaches it. The M4 soldier has no usable clip: `Enemy._animate_soldier` swings the legs from the walk phase and moves the gun by state (carry / aim / reload / club); both hands follow it through two-bone IK (`CharModel.ik_arm`, `point_bone`). Its file is odd: skeleton in the Standing pose and a skin 19.5x off, so `_rebind_skins` rebuilds the true T-pose rest from the skin and re-binds the meshes.
- Death: clip models (agile, people) play `death` turned along the shot; the others tip over with `tilt` and drop their arms (`_pose_limp`). The weapon is hidden at once (it drops as a pick-up). A settled body calls `CharModel.stop()`.
- **Hit boxes follow the skeleton (clip models: rifleman, blade, marksman, gunner).** `CharModel.hit_specs(body_scale)` builds 11 boxes from `CharModel.HIT_PARTS`, each stretched between two bones found by role (`CharModel.roles`, from the bone name's second `_` word: Hips, Spine, Neck, Head, HeadTop, LeftArm ... RightFoot): head (sphere r 0.17, `part` "head"), chest and pelvis ("body", boxes), upper arms and forearms ("arms", capsules), thighs and shins ("legs", capsules). Each is a top-level `Area3D` (layer 8, world metres, no rig scale) that `Enemy._sync_hitboxes()` puts at `CharModel.bone_world(bone, local)` after every animation step (also after the far-LOD step), so a shot lands where the body is mid-run, mid-crouch or mid-fall. `Enemy._bone_boxes` holds {area, bone, local}; a dead body frees them. Damage multipliers are unchanged and read from the box's `part` meta in `Weapons`: head x `head_mult`, legs x0.75, body and arms x1. The M4 soldier has no clips and keeps three static boxes in `hit_root` (legs, body, head; squashed to `SIT_HIT_SCALE` while he sits; the marksman's old 74% crouch squash is gone, his boxes follow the crouch clip). Legs and arms are thin now (a shot down the middle of the body at 0.4 m can pass between the legs). `tilt` is the node the model hangs under (lean, winded bend, tip-over).
- **Blending (`CharModel.play`).** Cross-fade time depends on the pair (`blend_time`: into a shot 0.1 s, between gaits 0.28, into aim / reload 0.16 / 0.18, otherwise 0.3; melee and death 0.06; death from a hit 0.12). Gait clips (`GAITS`: walk, run, crouch_walk, run_shoot, strafes, back, look) hand the stride phase over, so walk -> run does not slide a foot. A clip stays at least `HOLD_TICKS` (24 physics ticks = 0.2 s) before a non-urgent change (death, melee, reload and hit always go through), so aim / shoot / run do not flicker when the state wobbles. An explicit `blend` argument still wins.
- **Invader armour kit (`scripts/invader_armour.gd`, `Enemy._build_model`).** Every kind gets the same rust-plated kit so the five read as one army: octagonal helmet with a visor slit (accent colour per kind: orange, violet for the blade, red for the marksman, amber for the gunner), crest, jaw guard, pauldrons, chest plate with gorget and back pack, forearm bracers; `weight` makes the gunner bulky and the blade lean. Landmarks come from `CharModel.anchor_bone / anchor_pos` so one definition fits all three rigs. All plates of a soldier are ONE skinned vertex-coloured mesh (plus a visor mesh), hidden beyond `InvaderArmour.SHOW_DIST` 55 m. Visual only; hit boxes are unchanged. `LETHE_NOARMOUR=1` switches it off. COST NOT YET RE-MEASURED after the switch to a skinned mesh: the first version (a node per plate) cost about +8 ms with 80 soldiers in view; compare with `perf_anim.gd` and `LETHE_NOARMOUR=1`. Judged on stills only; nobody has seen it in motion.
- **Animation update-rate LOD (`Enemy._anim_interval`, `CharModel.lod_tick`).** The clips of a soldier 12 m or more from the player are not advanced by the AnimationPlayer every frame: its `callback_mode_process` goes to MANUAL and the enemy's physics tick calls `advance()` with all the elapsed time every 0.033 s (35 m and beyond), 0.066 s (70 m) or 0.125 s (120 m); inside 35 m, a soldier out of the camera's view is stepped at 0.125 s, one in view runs on his own. Cross-fades and loops are unchanged, only the steps are bigger. The bone hit boxes follow the same rate (`_sync_hitboxes`; `force = true` skips it), so a shot at a walking soldier 120 m away can be off by one step of his walk (0.25 m), inside the aim spread at that range. A death puts the player back on automatic (`_die`). Anything that stops an enemy's physics tick (a test, `process_mode` disabled) also freezes his animation while he is stepped by hand: call `model.lod_tick(0.0, 0.0)` first. `LETHE_NOANIMLOD=1` switches it off for A/B runs (numbers in [performance.md](performance.md): -6% frame time with 80 soldiers in view, so the animation was not the bottleneck there).
- **Pose layer (`scripts/pose_layer.gd`, `PoseLayer`, a `SkeletonModifier3D` child of the skeleton, agile + marksman only).** Runs after the AnimationPlayer and rotates spine / neck / head / arms / legs about skeleton-space axes (works on every rig): `flinch(part, dir, power)` from `Enemy.take_hit` (body: torso shoved along the blow with the head lagging; head: neck and head whip; arms: shoulders twist; legs: knees buckle; decays in about 0.3 s; the old whole-body `tilt` flinch is only used by the M4 soldier), `kick()` from `_fire_bolt` (shoulders back, head stays on target) and `takedown(k, sign)`. The enemy calls `layer.step(delta)` every physics tick (also while dead).
- **Takedown pair.** Victim: `Enemy.takedown(from)` kills at once (state DEAD, hit boxes freed, counted) but a clip model struggles for `TAKEDOWN_STRUGGLE` 0.55 s first: head yanked back, back arched, arms thrown up, knees giving (attacker behind him, `_td_sign` +1) or doubled over (attacker in front, -1); then `_dead_step` starts the `death` clip (it falls backwards, toward the attacker). Models without clips just fall as before. Attacker: the viewmodel (`Weapons`, `TAKEDOWN_TIME` 0.6 s): the left arm (`grab_arm`) clamps in front of the view, the knife is drawn back, thrusts, and both lower him; `Weapons._do_takedown` passes the player's position.
