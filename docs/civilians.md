# Civilians (civilian.gd, main.gd, enemy.gd)
Read this when you touch civilian.gd, the crowd, the route bodies or soldier-vs-civilian behaviour.
- Model: a person from the people pack (`CharModel` "people", ONE of 8 outfits picked by `look_seed`, so a seed always gives the same person), height and width vary, children are the same models at 70% size. Clips: Idle_Neutral, Walk, Run, HitRecieve (flinch when hit and alive), Death. Crouching (HIDE) squashes / leans the model. Animations of people farther than 70 m are paused (`ANIM_DIST`). Body on layer `Game.L_CIVILIAN` (16, collides with the world only), hit boxes `Area3D` on layer 8 with meta `part`, `civilian` = true and `owner_civ`. 40 health, no gore. `Civilian.spawn(parent, player, pos, seed, child, adult)` (position set before `add_child`).
- PANIC: run at 5.5 m/s along `City.path` to a goal at least 80 m from the nearest soldier (`last_pick_soldier_dist`), then another; while a soldier is in sight (`soldier_seen`) never a goal on a wide avenue. HIDE: a soldier with a clear line within 25 m, or a `Game.gunshot` (explosions emit one) within 20 m, sends him hiding 50% of the time: `find_hide()` = behind the nearest car (away from the threat) or at a building wall within 25 m; crouches 5-15 s, then PANIC. FOLLOW: a child (`leader`) stays within 2 m of his adult, runs alone when the adult dies. They step aside when the player is within 2.4 m and push away from each other.
- Soldiers vs civilians (`Enemy._civilian_fire`): a WANDER soldier with a gun (not the blade fighter, not the marksman) who sees a civilian within 30 m shoots at him (normal bursts, 7 degrees spread, hits through the civilian hit boxes, no `Game.gunshot`). The WANDER branch checks `_notices_player()` first, and `_aggravate` clears `civ_target`, so the player always comes first.
- Death: `take_hit` / `die(count, impulse, part)`: plays the Death clip turned so his head ends up along the shot (headshot: 1.6x speed; impulse > `BLAST_IMPULSE` 10 (grenade): a 0.7 s tumble about the chest first, then the clip), stays as a body in the SAME list and cap as enemy bodies (`Enemy.bodies`, `Enemy.add_body`; soldiers only get suspicious of Enemy bodies), `Game.civilians_lost` (end screens, checkpoints). The player hitting one (bullet, blade, grenade): toast "CIVILIAN HIT" (`Weapons.notice`), the mission goes on. Scenery bodies use `lie_down()` (not counted).
- Population (`Main._populate_civilians`, story only, before `mission.begin()` so the first checkpoint has them): about 50 living (9 of them children, each with an adult) in `City.ROUTE_ZONE`, 8 bodies within 30 m of `City.INSTITUTE_EXIT` and about 10 more lying in doorways and gutters along the way home (`CityStory.body_spots`). Nobody is added later. `Civilian.clock` (advanced by Main) vs each one's `leave_t` (random in the first 300 s): after it he picks goals far from the player and is removed when more than 150 m away and out of sight (camera frustum + clear line). Checkpoints store the living (with child links) and the dead civilians and rebuild them.
- LOD: `_update_lod` (every 0.1 s, staggered) decides drawn (HIDE_DIST), shadows (only within `SHADOW_DIST` 32 m), head + feet meshes (hidden beyond `FAR_DETAIL` 110 m: a person is 4 meshes, 10-12 surfaces, 7k vertices, 79 bones) and animation; the push away from other civilians is recomputed every 0.1 s (it loops over all of them), paths every `REPATH_TIME` 2.5 s; between `MID_DIST` 70 m and 150 m he updates every 0.05 s, beyond 150 m every 0.2 s with a scaled step (`lod_slow`, `updates` counts full updates), beyond 230 m he is hidden. Crowd ambience: `Sfx` `crowd` (6 s loop of band-filtered noise bursts, no voices) played by `Main._update_crowd`, louder with more civilians within 45 m, silent when none.
